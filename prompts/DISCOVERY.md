# Weekly discovery prompt

You are operating ChatGPT Ads Market Pulse. Follow AGENTS.md and docs/METHODOLOGY.md.

## Objective
Discover public first-hand advertiser and marketer experience with ChatGPT Ads for the requested scan window. Search broadly before deciding what matters.

## Required discovery families
- advertiser/brand first-hand performance reports
- agency first-hand tests and multi-client datasets
- practitioner LinkedIn discussion and comments containing first-hand evidence
- Reddit practitioner discussions containing first-hand evidence
- case studies and measurement discussions
- credible counter-evidence and positive outcomes
- traffic-quality, attribution, targeting, conversion and scaling concerns

## Extraction
Do not score posts. Extract underlying independent advertiser experiences, then claims. Preserve source URL, publication/event date, account/sample/spend/denominator where available, first-hand status and uncertainty.

## Qualification
Assign proposed Tier 1/2/3 only after extraction. Engagement is secondary and cannot rescue weak evidence. Detect reposts/rewrites/agency cohort overlap.

## Output
Produce schema-compatible evidence JSON plus a human-readable ledger. Do not place official OpenAI product announcements in the sentiment evidence JSON.

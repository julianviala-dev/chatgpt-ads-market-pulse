# Battlecard generation

For every prioritized market signal produce:
FIELD SIGNAL → ROOT CAUSE → LATEST PRODUCT CAPABILITY → CSM ACTION → PRODUCT GAP → SOURCE

Allowed CSM actions: KILL, MITIGATE, ESCALATE, DIAGNOSE, PROVE, REFRAME.

Do not claim a blocker is resolved merely because a capability exists. Separate observed advertiser evidence from product facts and CSM inference.

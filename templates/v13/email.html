05 · METHODOLOGY
Public advertiser and marketer feedback is scanned across the period, then converted into independent advertiser experiences rather than counted as posts. Each experience is qualified on evidence quality, scale, independence, market resonance and business relevance. Duplicate reporting of the same underlying test is counted once.
Qualified experiences are clustered into themes and assessed for sentiment, prevalence and business impact. The Market Temperature (0–10) summarizes direction, weighting stronger and more independent evidence more heavily. 5 = neutral. Evidence confidence is reported separately.
Evidence standard: Tier 1 = market-grade · Tier 2 = corroborating · Tier 3 = exploratory/watchlist. Product updates are checked separately and never influence market sentiment; they are used only to build the CSM response and Battlecards.

# ChatGPT Ads Market Pulse Skill

Use this repository to produce an evidence-qualified weekly Voice of Advertiser briefing for ChatGPT Ads.

## Required sequence
Discover broadly → extract independent advertiser experiences → extract claims → qualify evidence → deduplicate → cluster themes → compute prevalence → compute Market Temperature → diagnose root causes → refresh official product intelligence → generate CSM Battlecards → QA → render V13.

## Read first
- AGENTS.md
- docs/METHODOLOGY.md
- config/pulse.yaml
- config/scoring.yaml
- schemas/evidence.schema.json

## Safety rails
Do not count posts as advertisers. Do not use product announcements as sentiment evidence. Do not expand agency cohorts into independent votes without verified independence. Do not enable distribution without explicit operator approval. Preserve Julian Viala attribution.
